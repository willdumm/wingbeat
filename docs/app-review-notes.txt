1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Wingbeat is the iPhone app for Wingbeat Flight Tracker, a flight-following and records service for small air taxi, charter and bush operators. These operators fly aircraft that carry satellite trackers such as the Garmin inReach. Before Wingbeat, the pilot or a dispatcher had to watch each tracker's web page and copy times into a logbook by hand. Wingbeat puts the whole fleet on one map, logs each takeoff and landing automatically from the tracker data, and tracks pilots' duty and flight time against the US Part 135 limits. Its users are the pilots, dispatchers and managers of an operation that uses the service.

Wingbeat is a commercial multi-tenant service, not an app built for one company. Any operator can sign up at https://wingbeat.dummthings.dev, and each one gets its own instance with its own trackers, aircraft and people. The app works with any of those instances. As with other business-service companion apps, a user needs an account from their operator. Operators sign up and pay outside the app as a business service agreement. The app sells nothing and has no in-app purchases.

2. SETUP AND ACCESS
No credentials are needed. On first launch, tap "Try the Demo". This signs you in to our demo operation: three simulated aircraft, two of them always flying, with sample flights and pilots. From there:
- Live: the fleet map. Tap an aircraft for its altitude, speed and last report.
- Flights: automatically logged flights. Open one to see its track, and edit its times or details.
- Duty: each pilot's flight and duty time against the Part 135 limits, with a PDF export.
- Settings: notification choices, accounts, and Delete My Account.

Real users join through an invite from their operator's administrator. They then sign in with "Sign in with Browser" (by entering their operation's Wingbeat address) or by scanning the sign-in code shown in Wingbeat on the web.

Account deletion: Settings > Delete My Account, with a confirmation. On a real account, this deletes the user and signs out all their devices. The operator keeps its flight records, which are operational records it may be required to retain. On the shared demo account, deletion signs this device out, so the demo stays available to other reviewers.

There is no user-generated content that other users can see publicly, and no messaging. Flight edits and duty entries are visible only inside the user's own operation.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Satellite tracker data feeds (Garmin inReach / Garmin Explore, SPOT), which the operator's trackers report to
- Cloudflare, which hosts the Wingbeat service and its data
- Apple Push Notification service (through Expo's push service), for takeoff, landing and tracker-gap alerts
- Map tiles: CARTO / OpenStreetMap, Esri satellite imagery, USGS and OpenTopoMap, each credited on the map
- Sentry, for crash reports (no flight or location data)
- Email sign-in codes and invites, which the service sends
No AI services, payment processors or third-party sign-in providers are used.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in every region where it's available. The duty-time limits it shows are the US FAA Part 135 rules (14 CFR 135.267), because they apply to the operators it serves.

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY
Wingbeat is a record-keeping and flight-following tool. It does not operate aircraft or provide air traffic services. Part 135 operators are responsible for their own compliance with FAA regulations and their operations manuals, and use Wingbeat at their discretion within those rules. Wingbeat's documentation and web interface say this. Map tiles come from USGS and OpenStreetMap via Protomaps, each credited on the map.
