# Settings

Opened with **Settings** on the dashboard or analytics page. Settings fills the
screen, with a sidebar of pages grouped by who each setting applies to:

- **This browser**: Appearance and Storage, kept on this device only.
- **Your account**: Account (devices, email addresses), which follows you to
  every device you sign in on.
- **The instance**, under its name: shared by everyone on it. Everyone sees
  Aircraft, Pilots, Trackers and Export flights; only Admins see the other
  pages, and only Admins can make changes.

Each page's heading repeats its group as a tag. A page has its own address
(`#settings/trackers`, for example), so a link can open it and the browser's Back
button leaves it. On a phone the sidebar is the first screen, and a page slides
in over it.

In a list (trackers, users, devices, basemaps…) each entry's actions are in its
**⋯** menu. Clicking an entry opens its edit form, or what it leads to (an
aircraft's details, a pilot's duty log).

- **[User settings](user-settings)**: the pages every signed-in user has.
- **[Admin settings](admin-settings)**: the instance pages only Admins see.

See [Users & Devices](../features/users-and-devices) for how roles and
invites work.
